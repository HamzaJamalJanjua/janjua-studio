Janjua Studio is a small private desktop tool owned by Hamza Jamal Janjua. It posts the short videos he makes for his own YouTube channels to his own TikTok accounts on a schedule, using the official TikTok for Developers APIs (Login Kit and the Content Posting API).

How it works: the owner signs in with TikTok once on his own computer and approves the requested permissions. After that the tool uploads each finished short video with its caption to the connected TikTok account. Access tokens stay on the owner's computer.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
