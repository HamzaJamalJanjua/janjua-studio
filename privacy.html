Last updated: 1 October 2026

Janjua Studio is a private desktop publishing tool. It is used only by its owner, Hamza Jamal Janjua, to publish short videos that he produced himself from his own YouTube channels to his own TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses. When the owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives the permissions he approves: basic profile information (display name and avatar) and permission to upload and publish videos to his own account. The tool uses this only to show which account is connected and to post his videos.

What is stored. Access tokens are kept only on the owner's own computer, in a local settings file, so that scheduled posts can run. No TikTok data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

Other people's data. The tool does not collect, read or store any data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

Deleting data. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time in TikTok settings under Security and permissions, Manage app permissions, and can delete the local settings file. Revoking access stops all posting immediately.
